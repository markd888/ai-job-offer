# /today — heads-up checks: shape mismatch, coach nudge, profile gaps

<!-- Loaded on demand by skills/today/SKILL.md; part of /today, split out to keep the always-loaded skill small. -->

Read when one of the conditions listed under `## Heads-up` in the skill holds. Each entry gives the full trigger, the precedence between triggers and the wording.

- **Shape-mismatch warning on active interviews.** Trigger: any meta.md with `status: interviewing` AND (`team_size > 150` OR the company body/research-brief mentions ">150 ppl") AND the company is NOT in any `target_industries` entry from profile.md AND no explicit equity / brand signal in research-brief. When triggered: `Shape mismatch — <Company> is interviewing but <signal recap>. Re-read the role-fit verdict before next round.` This is the hollowing-risk check: a big-co interview proceeding without the shape signals the user actually wants. Skip if no qualifying meta.
- **Coach nudge.** Surfaces when a multi-week pattern suggests `job-offer:career-coach` would help diagnose. Three independent triggers — fire at most ONE per /today run. **Trigger C is highest severity; if C fires, suppress A and B.** Skip if no trigger fires.
  - **Trigger A — cadence drift.** Weekly targets (`applications` AND/OR `warm_outreach`) missed by >50% for 3 weeks running. Compute by counting `date_applied` per 7-day window AND `sent` outreach lines in `userdata/people/*.md` logs per window across the last 21 days. When triggered: `Cadence under 50% three weeks running — job-offer:career-coach can help work out if the targets or the search itself needs a rethink.`
  - **Trigger B — passing without applying.** ≥3 meta.md files have `status: not_interested` AND `strategy.md ## Anti-goals` section body is empty. (User is passing on roles without applying — pattern suggests an unstated rule worth naming.) When triggered: `Passed on <N> roles without applying — there's probably a pattern worth naming. Ask job-offer:career-coach to walk you through it.`
  - **Trigger C — sunk-cost / structural rethink.** Highest-severity trigger. Suppresses A and B if it fires. Three OR-conditions, ANY one fires it:
    1. **Long search, thin pipeline.** weeks-since-`/setup` ≥ 8 (compute from the earliest `date_added` across all meta.md OR from `target_offer_date` if available) AND companies in `status: interviewing` count < 2 AND companies in `status: offer` count = 0.
    2. **Pattern-of-rejection.** `rejection-patterns.py` prints `← repeated stage`: 3+ rejections at the same stage past the application (`screening`, `interview` or `final`). Rejections at `application` are silence, not a stage pattern.
    3. **Cadence-drift escalation.** Trigger A has fired in 4+ consecutive `/today` runs (would require persistent state; if not implementable, fall back to: weeks-since-/setup ≥ 4 AND weekly targets missed by >50% for 4 weeks running).

    When triggered, the message must **cite specific data** (which counts, which dates, which stage) — not abstract framing. Examples:
    - Condition 1: `9 weeks since /setup, 1 active interview thread, 0 offers. This isn't a cadence problem — job-offer:career-coach can help work out whether the strategy itself needs restarting, not tweaking.`
    - Condition 2: `3 rejections at the <stage> stage. Same stage, different companies — job-offer:career-coach can help work out what specifically is happening there.`
    - Condition 3: `Cadence under 50% for 4 weeks running. The targets or the search shape probably need a structural change, not another push — job-offer:career-coach can help diagnose.`
- **Monthly rejection review.** Not a coach-nudge trigger; it gets its own line and never suppresses or is suppressed by A–C. Trigger, all three:
  - 5+ roles with `status: rejected` whose `date_rejected` (or the History line's date) is after `last_rejection_review` in strategy.md, or 5+ rejections in all when there's no review yet;
  - 30+ days since `last_rejection_review`, or none recorded;
  - not surfaced in the last 7 days (same marker-file convention as the weekly reflection, `userdata/outputs/.last-rejection-review-prompt`).

  Run `python3 -I "${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/scripts/rejection-patterns.py" --userdata "$(pwd)/userdata"` (with `--since <last_rejection_review>` when set) and quote its strongest line: the first `← repeated stage`, else a low reply rate, else instant rejections. When it prints `Patterns: 0`, quote the count only. Wording: `**Rejections** — <N> since <date|you started>; <strongest line, e.g. "5 at the interview stage" or "P0 roles: 1 reply in 22">. → ask job-offer:career-coach for a rejection review.` Never call a pattern a reason.
- **Profile gaps.** Trigger: `## Proof Points` or `## Moat` in `userdata/profile.md` is empty AND at least one role is filed AND `/setup` did not run during this session. `/setup` deliberately stops at the positioning paragraph, so these sections start empty on every install; this is where they get picked up, once the user has seen real roles and can write a proof point against a real job description. Fire at most one per run, and at most once every 7 days — track via the same marker-file convention as the weekly reflection. When triggered: `**Your profile** — no proof points saved yet, so tailored CVs have nothing to draw on → tell me two wins and I'll write them up, or ask job-offer:career-coach to sharpen the whole positioning with you.`
